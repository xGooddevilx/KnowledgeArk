### Ability: Can, Know How to, and Be Able To:

#### Can:

- is used to express physical ability, as in (a).
- is used with verbs of the five senses (see,hear,...)
- Negative: Cannot, Can't, Can not.

> e.g:
>
> - Tome is strong, He can lift that heavy box.
> - I can see the park from here.
> - He can't wait any longer.

#### Know how to:

- Can and Know how to, are used to express a learned skill.
- Know how to do = Can do

> e.g:
>
> - Maria Knows how to play this game.
> - I know how to code = I can code.

#### Be able to:

- expresses the ability
- **Not Able to** may also be expressed as **unable**.
- be able to do = can do

> e.g:
>
> - I ame able to help you now.
> - Are you able to help me I lift this ?
> - I am unable (not able to) to help you.
