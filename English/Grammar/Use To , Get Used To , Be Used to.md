### Used To

> We use this term to tell about an addiction or anything routing that we had done in the past but not any more in present or future.

Formula: Subject + used to + verb (base form)

e.g:

- I used to night owl, but these days i sleep before 10;
- They used to win all the match, but they not this year.

### Be Used To

> in order to show that speaker / subject being familiar with something or a routine activity.

Formula: Subject + (am/is/are/was/were) + used to + verb-ing / noun

e.g:

- I am used to my hair losing.
- She hates the traffic noises, but she is used to it now.

### Get Used To:

> To show the process of accustom (result is in future)

Formula: Subject + (get/gets/got/getting) + used to + verb-ing / noun

e.g:

- He didn't like new home, but he is getting used to it.
- Don't smoke to much, you will get used to it.
