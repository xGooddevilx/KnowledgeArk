### Out of:

- #### To have no more of something:

    > We are out of gas, I ran out of milk.

- #### From inside something:

    > Magician, took a rabbit out of his hat. , Move your hands out of the car.

- #### Because of / motivated by:

    > He yelled out of anger. , They cried out of joy.

- #### From a total number:
    > Four out of 10 students. , Two team qualified out of 32 teams in the tournament.
